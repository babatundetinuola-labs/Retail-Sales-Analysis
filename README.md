RETAIL SALES ANALYSIS

Project Overview
 This project analyses a retail sales dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and monthly trends.

 The analysis was completed using Microsoft Excel, with data cleaning, calculations, Pivottables, PivotCHarts, and an interactive-style dashboard.

 DATASET

  The dataset contains 500 retail orders and includes information such as:

  Order ID
  Order Date
  Customer
  Product
  Category
  Region
  Quantity
  Unit Price
  Discount
  Sales
  Profit
  Payment Method

  TOOLS USED

  Microsoft Excel
  PivotTables
  PivotCharts
  Excel Formulas
  Data cleaning and validation
  Dashboard creation

  KEY RESULTS

  Total Sales: #258,831,300
  Total Profit: #63,604,368
  Total Orders: 500
  Total Quantity Sold: 1,501 units
  Average Sales Per order: #517,662.60

  KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS

  REGIONAL PERFORMANCE 

  Abuja recorded the highest sales at #60,974,400, followed by Oyo, Rivers, Lagos, and Kano.

  However, Oyo recorded the highest profit among the regions. This shows that sales revenue alone does not provide a complete picture of regional performance.

  PRODUCT PERFORMANCE 

  The laptop pro 14 was the highest-selling product by sales value, generating #94,542,500 in sales.

  The smartphone X was the seconded-highest-selling product at #60,186,000.

  MONTHLY PERFORMANCE 

  Monthly sales and profit were analyzed to help identify periods of stronger and weaker business performance.

  RECOMMENDATIONS

  1. Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing products such as the laptop Pro 14 and smartphone X.
  2. Investigate the factors behind Abuja's strong sales performance and consider applying successful strategies to other regions.
  3. Pay attention to profitability, not only sales revenue, when evaluating regional performance.
  4. Investigate weaker-performing region such as Kano to understand whether marketing, demand, pricing, or product availability is affecting performance.
  5. Use monthly sales and profit trends to improve inventory and promotional planning.
